# Requirements Spec: Weekly Distribution Ops Reporting Pack

| **Requestor** | Director, Distribution Operations (portfolio scenario) |
| **Users** | Distribution Ops leadership, regional channel managers, Sales Ops, Finance (billing) |
| **Analyst / owner** | Rishitha Bejjanki |
| **Status** | v1.0 delivered |

## 1. Business need
Leadership reviews backlog, billing, and distributor performance every Monday. Today the numbers are assembled by hand from several systems, take about half a day, and occasionally disagree with Finance. They need one consistent, automated weekly pack.

## 2. Business questions the pack must answer
1. How did backlog move this week, and why (new bookings vs shipments)?
2. How much backlog is past due, in which product families, and is it getting better or worse?
3. Are we billing this quarter at a faster or slower pace than last quarter?
4. Are we shipping on time against our commit dates and against customer request dates?
5. Which distributors are healthy, and which need attention?

## 3. KPI definitions (agreed with stakeholders)
| KPI | Definition | Grain | Source |

| Bookings | Σ order qty × unit price, by **order date** | order line | FACT_ORDERS |
| Shipments | Σ shipped qty × order unit price, by **ship date** | shipment | FACT_SHIPMENTS |
| Billings | Σ invoice amount, by **invoice date** | invoice line | FACT_BILLING |
| Backlog (end of week) | Opening backlog + Σ bookings − Σ shipments through week end | distributor × family × week | derived |
| Delinquent backlog | Open value of lines whose scheduled ship date is before week end and not fully shipped | order line × week | derived |
| QTD pace | QTD billings ÷ prior quarter QTD billings at the same week of quarter − 1 | quarter × week of quarter | derived |
| OTD to commit | Lines **fully** shipped within 3 days after scheduled ship date ÷ lines due | order line, by commit month | derived |
| OTD to request | Same, measured against customer request date | order line | derived |
| Weeks of inventory | Distributor on-hand units ÷ 13-week average POS units, at month-end week | distributor × month | Project 1 view |
| Health status | Red: WOI > 14 or OTD < 80%; Amber: WOI > 11 or OTD < 90%; else Green | distributor × month | derived |

## 4. Business rules and decisions
- **Partial shipments:** a line counts as on time only when the full quantity has shipped inside the window (agreed with Ops; stricter than "first shipment").
- **Grace window:** 3 days after commit date, to absorb carrier pickup timing.
- **Open lines past due** at reporting time count as late in OTD.
- **Shipment value** uses the order unit price (not invoice price) so price errors don't distort backlog.
- **Excluded:** nothing in v1. End-of-life parts exclusion is a v2 request (see Project 5).

## 5. Data and refresh
- Sources: Snowflake `RAW` tables (orders, shipments, billing, POS, inventory) + `MART` views from Project 1.
- Refresh: weekly, Monday 6am, after the Sunday data load.
- Output: Excel pack (7 tabs) generated by `build_report_pack.py`; Power BI page optional.

## 6. Acceptance criteria
- [x] Waterfall ending backlog ties to the current backlog view to the dollar.
- [x] Each week's beginning backlog equals the prior week's ending backlog.
- [x] Latest-week delinquency ties to current delinquent backlog.
- [x] Scorecard billings tie to source billings.
- [x] Pack is **not published** if any tie-out fails.
- [x] Every KPI in section 3 is documented, with its source and grain.

## 8. Risks and mitigations
| Risk | Mitigation |

| Finance and Ops define billings differently | Definitions agreed in section 3; pack ties to source billing table |
| Late data load on Monday | Pack checks the latest week before running; tie-out gate blocks publishing |
| Fiscal vs calendar quarter mismatch | v1 uses calendar quarters; fiscal calendar is an open item for v2 |
